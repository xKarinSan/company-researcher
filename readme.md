# Company Research Agent

### 1. Basic Company Information
- Identify the company’s full name and headquarters location.
- Determine the year it was founded.
- Look up the company's mission statement and core values.

### 2. Business Overview
- Understand what products/services the company offers.
- Identify the industry and market segments it operates in.
- Review its business model (e.g., B2B, B2C, SaaS, etc.).

### 3. Leadership and Structure
- Research key executives (CEO, CFO, etc.) and board members.
- Check for recent leadership changes.
- Understand the company’s organizational structure if available.

### 4. Financial Performance
- Review recent financial reports (e.g., annual reports, quarterly earnings).
- Look at key financial metrics (revenue, profit, debt, etc.).
- Check stock performance (if public) and analyst ratings.

### 5. Market Position and Competitors
- Identify the company’s main competitors.
- Compare market share, pricing, and customer base.
- Understand its competitive advantages or disadvantages.

### 6. Recent News and Developments
- Search for recent news articles or press releases.
- Note any major events (mergers, acquisitions, lawsuits, etc.).
- Look at new product launches or strategic changes.

### 7. Company Culture and Reputation
- Read employee reviews (Glassdoor, Indeed).
- Check for awards or recognition related to workplace culture.
- Investigate any controversies or public relations issues.

### 8. Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)
- Review sustainability initiatives.
- Check for community involvement and charitable activities.
- See if the company has any ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance) ratings.

### 9. Customer Feedback and Brand Perception
- Analyze customer reviews (Google, Yelp, Trustpilot, etc.).
- Look at social media sentiment.
- Evaluate brand recognition and loyalty.

### 10. SWOT Analysis (Optional Summary)
- Summarize Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats.
- This helps consolidate findings into strategic insights.
